Independent Label

AfterimageAssembly

Afterimage Assembly is Jonathan Kendall's independent label, for music that lingers: works designed not for immediate impact, but for resonance over time.

Albums are treated as complete forms, with sequencing and atmosphere given as much weight as individual tracks.
